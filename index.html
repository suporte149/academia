Module 10 · Responsible technology

Governance, ethics & safeguards

Framework commitments are shown separately from policies still in preparation.

Framework · implementation in preparation

Human oversight of AI

AI suggests → participant acts → rubric informs → human evaluator verifies → passport updates.

Privacy / LGPD-ready

Data minimization, consent, access, correction, deletion, portability and explanation principles.

Safeguarding

Participant protection, escalation procedures and role-based access are due-diligence requirements.

Industrial safety

Machine authorization, supervised operation, training prerequisites and incident reporting.

Financial controls

Procurement thresholds, approvals, audit trail and segregation of duties.

Anti-fraud & conflicts

Declared-interest register, whistleblowing path and transparent partner/vendor selection.
